About关于

Over the past ten years, moving has meant leaving the familiar behind and learning how to find myself somewhere new. Each move asked me to choose what to keep, what to let go of, and what was worth carrying forward. In making those choices, I learned to understand myself, connect with others, and pay closer attention to what gives life meaning.

Every move shaped my design process before I knew how to name them. Packing taught me hierarchy. Letting go taught me discernment. Arriving taught me to listen before I speak.

Today, I bring those lessons into my practice by understanding the people, culture, and history behind a project before deciding what the design should become. I don’t design to replace what already exists. I translate the meaning people have woven into a place into form, material, and experience.

过去十年里，搬家意味着离开熟悉的一切，在新的地方重新找到自己。每一次搬家，都要我决定留下什么、放下什么、什么值得带走。在这些选择里，我学会了理解自己、与人建立联系，也更留意那些让生活有意义的东西。

在我能说清楚之前，每一次搬家就已经在塑造我的设计方法。打包教会我层级，放下教会我取舍，抵达教会我先倾听、再开口。

如今，我把这些带进自己的设计实践：在决定设计该成为什么之前，先去理解项目背后的人、文化与历史。我做设计，不是为了取代已经存在的东西，而是把人们编织进一个地方的意义，转化为形式、材料与体验。
